## Positioning
Partnership model. Recommended thesis (pending sign-off): *"AI-native operations. Human-led execution. Priced to what the work actually needs — not a tier."* Ties the AI-native/human-touch claim directly to the new pricing model rather than floating as a standalone value statement. Fully transparent, integrated team — not a black-box vendor relationship.

## Verticals served
Presented as equal on the homepage (full generalist positioning, deliberate choice):
- DTC (cannabis/CBD, health & wellness, etc.)
- E-commerce
- Startups (SaaS, fintech, tech)
- Restaurants & hospitality
- Sports & fitness
- Service-based businesses (contractors, concrete, carpet cleaners, painters, etc.)

Portfolio proof: service-based (Thread House Ink, Cooper Concrete, Royal Peacock), DTC (Energy Exchange), sports/fitness (Hitters Quarters), fintech, and restaurants/hospitality all have case study proof. General SaaS/tech within the startups vertical (outside fintech specifically) still needs a proof point.

## Process — Map / Ship / Multiply
Locked. Replaces Diagnose/Deploy/Compound — that wording belongs to a local competitor. The engagement narrative — how working with RVNW unfolds over time. Kept fully separate from pricing; this answers "what happens," not "what it costs."

- **Map** — grounded, data-informed diagnosis of the business
- **Ship** — decisive execution across the relevant disciplines, ties directly to the Dev/Engineering discipline without repeating its name
- **Multiply** — compounding value over time, without leaning on "scale" or "compound"

## Pricing model
Moved away from productized retainer tiers (Foundation/Framework/Momentum/Cornerstone) to value-oriented, project-based pricing. Custom proposals scoped to each client's actual needs — no fixed tiers, no over/under-charging. Keep an internal scoping framework consistent across proposals so pricing discipline doesn't erode without a tier structure enforcing it.

## Brand references
Nike, The North Face, DEPT. Nike and North Face inform tone and photographic energy — stark black/white, athletic-technical confidence, color used as signal. DEPT is the closer structural reference: a digital agency running big bold type, grid-based layout, and saturated color-blocking on black at award-grade execution — worth studying their actual page architecture, not just brand identity.

## Brand mark
Standalone geometric "R" monogram — chamfered/notched corners on the strokes, rounded bowl as the one soft counterpoint. The mark itself is the logo; no container. Icon/favicon is the bare R on transparent background. Corner radius across the design system should echo the chamfer language rather than defaulting to rounded/pill shapes.

## Typography
Public Sans (Semi Bold) for headings/buttons/menus, Instrument Sans for body copy, JetBrains Mono as a technical accent (stats, tags, labels) — not body text.

## Site requirements
- Homepage structure locked: Hero → Services/Disciplines → Verticals → Process → Our Work → CTA → Footer (full detail and reasoning in Homepage Section Map.md)
- Full-page hero sections used to drive animation and visually separate sections
- Bold, big type as the primary hierarchy tool — grid layouts throughout
- Social/branding assets: big bold type, bright colors, mixed with contrasting black-background assets

## Tech stack
Netlify, Git, Resend, Sanity CMS — an intentional split from RVNW's usual Next.js/Supabase/Vercel default, specific to this marketing/content-heavy site.
