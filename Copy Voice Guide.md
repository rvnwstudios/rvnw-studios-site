# RVNW Studios — Copy & Voice Guide

## Register
Senior operator talking to senior operators. Direct, confident, no fluff. This site is the credibility proof behind every client pitch RVNW makes — held to the strictest version of this voice, not a looser "marketing site" version of it.

Take positions. Don't hedge into "both sides have merit" copy. Use contractions — this isn't a formal-register brand.

## Banned vocabulary
delve, tapestry, landscape (metaphorical), navigate (metaphorical), leverage (verb), foster, robust, utilize, nuanced, multifaceted, pivotal, underscores, holistic, synergy, paradigm, transformative, groundbreaking, cutting-edge, harness, streamline, cornerstone, encompasses, facilitates, moreover, furthermore, nevertheless, myriad, plethora, ensures/ensures that, respective/respectively, unlock, elevate, seamless, game changer, "key" as an adjective ("key takeaways" → "what mattered")

## Banned patterns
- "It's not just X, it's Y" / any variant
- "Not only X, but also Y"
- "Whether you're a beginner or a seasoned pro..." false-choice filler
- Signposting: "Let's break this down," "Here's the thing," "Let's dive in"
- Filler: "at its core," "at the end of the day," "when it comes to," "the power of X"
- Openings: "In today's rapidly evolving...," "Imagine a world where...," "Picture this:"
- Hedges: "It's important to note...," "It's worth mentioning..."
- Generic closings, "In conclusion" summaries

## Banned filler constructions
- Hollow intensifiers (truly, really, incredibly, absolutely) — at most one per page, usually zero
- Paired synonyms ("clear and concise") — pick one word
- "Allows you to / enables you to / empowers you to" — say what the thing does directly
- Weasel quantifiers without numbers (significant, substantial, considerable) — quantify it or cut it

## Sentence-level
- Vary length aggressively — mix short punches with longer compound sentences. No three consecutive sentences within 5 words of each other in length.
- Default to active voice. "We built X," not "X was built."
- Let the point land at the end sometimes — not every sentence needs to lead with the claim.

## Length discipline
A button label is a button label. A stat callout is a few words. Match the length the format calls for — cut before adding.

## RVNW-specific notes
- Positioning line: *"AI-native operations. Human-led execution. Priced to what the work actually needs — not a tier."* — pending final sign-off, but this is the reference point for tone if drafting variations.
- Process copy uses **Map / Ship / Multiply** — verbs, in motion, parallel structure. Don't soften these into noun-phrases ("Mapping," "Our Ship Phase") in headlines — keep them as the punchy verb form.
- Never describe RVNW as a large team or agency-of-many — the "AI-native ops with human touch" positioning is honest about a lean, integrated operation. Overstating size undercuts the actual differentiator.
